# ArqAI /about Copy Revision: the ACI relationship

Replaces the current "Our parent / Backed by enterprise delivery depth" section on thearq.ai/about. Reframes the ACI relationship from parent-and-captive to a partnership, in line with the agreed positioning (two independent companies, strategic partnership, ACI as enterprise delivery and distribution partner). Protects the seed raise by removing the captive signal, while keeping the real credibility benefit.

## Replacement copy (ready to paste)

Eyebrow: Our partner

Heading: Independent studio. Enterprise reach.

Body paragraph 1:
ArqAI Labs is an independent AI engineering studio. We work in close partnership with ACI Infotech, a privately held enterprise technology firm, and that partnership gives us what most AI studios never get: direct access to enterprise operators, proven implementation playbooks, and the delivery depth to take AI all the way into production and keep it running.

Body paragraph 2:
Our founders built their careers inside enterprise operations through ACI. That is why we engineer for production from day one, and why we stand up governed AI inside real systems instead of stopping at a slide.

Kicker: Independent by design. Enterprise-deep by partnership.

## What changed and why

- "Our parent" becomes "Our partner." ArqAI is an independent company, not a subsidiary in public language.
- "Backed by ACI Infotech" and "Same team, all the way from strategy to run" are removed. Both read as captive, which is the exact signal that compresses an AI company's valuation in a raise.
- The shared-founders line carries the genuine ACI connection truthfully, so the relationship stays honest without making ArqAI look owned.
- The delivery and enterprise-access benefit is kept, because that is the real value of the partnership for buyers.

## Note

This should be applied alongside the matching fix on the ACI site, which currently calls ArqAI "our proprietary platform." Both sides need the partnership framing for the story to hold together. Founding year stays 2024 across the site until the legal date is confirmed.
